FREEGEMAS - APPLE IIGS EDITION V1.0
===================================

HOW TO PLAY
-----------
Swap adjacent gems to make horizontal or vertical groups of three or more.
Only swaps that make a match are accepted. Other swaps return to their
original positions. Cascades score additional points.

GAME MODES
----------
TIME TRIAL: Score as much as possible before the two-minute timer expires.
ENDLESS: Play without a timer. The game ends when no legal move remains.

CONTROLS
--------
TITLE: W/S or the Up/Down cursor keys move through the menu. Space or Enter
starts the selected item. The mouse can hover and click menu items.

GAME: W/A/S/D or the four cursor keys move the board selector. Space selects
a gem, then swaps it with an adjacent gem. With a mouse, click two adjacent
gems, or hold the first gem and drag to an adjacent gem to swap.

H       Show a legal move. The next scoring move earns no points.
R       Restart the current mode and reset its score and timer.
ESC     Return to the title screen.

The left-side Show Hint, Reset Game, and Exit buttons can also be clicked.
Options lets you turn Music and Sound on or off. Fullscreen is always on.
During Game Over, press Space or click the board to replay; press ESC to
return to the title screen.

HIGH SCORES
-----------
Time Trial and Endless records are stored in HISCORE.DAT on this disk. The
disk must be writable for new records to persist.
All game resources and high scores are in the DATA folder. Keep it beside
FREEGEM.S16 for the game to load correctly.
